Q4  [(Food ⇒ Party) ∨ (Drinks ⇒ Party)] ⇒ [(Food ∧ Drinks) ⇒ Party]
======================================================================

(a) Enumeration

Drinks | Food | Party | (Food ⇒ Party) ∨ (Drinks ⇒ Party) | (Food ∧ Drinks) ⇒ Party | Sentence
-------+------+-------+-----------------------------------+-------------------------+---------
T      | T    | T     | T                                 | T                       | T       
T      | T    | F     | F                                 | F                       | T       
T      | F    | T     | T                                 | T                       | T       
T      | F    | F     | T                                 | T                       | T       
F      | T    | T     | T                                 | T                       | T       
F      | T    | F     | T                                 | T                       | T       
F      | F    | T     | T                                 | T                       | T       
F      | F    | F     | T                                 | T                       | T       

=> VALID

(b) CNF of each side

  Left-hand side:
    Original                                                (Food ⇒ Party) ∨ (Drinks ⇒ Party)
    2. Eliminate ⇒: α ⇒ β ≡ ¬α ∨ β                          (¬Food ∨ Party) ∨ (¬Drinks ∨ Party)
    5. Simplify: drop repeated literals and tautologies     ¬Drinks ∨ ¬Food ∨ Party

  Right-hand side:
    Original                                                (Food ∧ Drinks) ⇒ Party
    2. Eliminate ⇒: α ⇒ β ≡ ¬α ∨ β                          ¬(Food ∧ Drinks) ∨ Party
    3. Move ¬ inwards (De Morgan, double negation)          (¬Food ∨ ¬Drinks) ∨ Party
    5. Simplify: drop repeated literals and tautologies     ¬Drinks ∨ ¬Food ∨ Party

(c) Resolution: prove the sentence is valid by showing ¬(sentence) is unsatisfiable
     1. Food                             (from ¬sentence in CNF)
     2. Drinks                           (from ¬sentence in CNF)
     3. ¬Drinks ∨ ¬Food ∨ Party          (from ¬sentence in CNF)
     4. ¬Party                           (from ¬sentence in CNF)
     5. ¬Drinks ∨ ¬Food                  (resolve 3 and 4)
     6. ¬Food                            (resolve 2 and 5)
     7. { }  (empty clause)              (resolve 1 and 6)

=> empty clause derived: True  ->  the sentence is VALID
